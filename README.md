## Overview

1eye turns buying signals into qualified buyers in Twenty.

1eye identifies people showing buying intent across three channels: Website (who's visiting), Market (who's researching), and LinkedIn (who's engaging). Its AI identifies and enriches buyers, then matches them against your Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) and Personas.

Connect 1eye to Twenty to automatically sync qualified buyers and their companies into your CRM. Give sales and marketing teams the right prospects, enriched contact information, and actionable buying intelligence, without manual exports or unqualified lead lists.

## How it works

1eye captures buying signals across Website, Market, and LinkedIn, identifies the people behind them, and qualifies buyers against your ICPs and Personas. Qualified buyers are organized into Target Lists, which determine who gets synced to Twenty.

The integration automatically:

- Creates or updates People and Company records.
- Matches existing People by email and Companies by domain to prevent unnecessary duplicates.
- Associates buyers with their respective companies.
- Syncs enriched contact and company information using configurable standard and custom field mappings.
- Supports custom fields for ICPs, Personas, and buying signals.

Choose which Target Lists to sync and when workflows run. Monitor Pushed, Pending, and Failed records directly in the 1eye Console. Only your configured fields are synced, and existing unrelated Twenty data is preserved.

## Configuration

1. **Create your account:** Sign up at 1eye.ai or sign in to your existing 1eye workspace.
2. **Define your buyers:** Configure your ICPs, Personas, and Target Lists in 1eye.
3. **Add Twenty:** Go to Destinations → Add Destination, create a workflow, and select Twenty.
4. **Connect your workspace:** Click Connect and authorize your Twenty workspace.
5. **Configure field mappings:** Review the default People and Company fields. Enable or disable standard fields and map additional buyer information to custom Twenty fields.
6. **Start syncing:** Save your Destination, select your Target Lists, and run the workflow once, continuously, or until a specified date.
7. **Monitor results:** Track successful, pending, and failed syncs in the 1eye Console.
